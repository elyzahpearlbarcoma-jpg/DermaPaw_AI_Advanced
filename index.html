                      SET THE RIGHT EXPECTATIONS

                      What DermaPaw AI can and cannot do

                      What it can do

                      • Accept pet skin images for screening and show actual model classification results when the model is connected and working.
                      • Explain possible meanings of visible patterns and provide general educational care guidance.
                      • Describe possible veterinary approaches as education, not as automatic treatment recommendations.
                      • Guide users through applicable observation steps and record care notes, observations, and progress photos in local browser storage.
                      • Help owners review visible changes using saved notes and photo comparisons.

                      What it cannot do

                      • Confirm a disease or replace veterinary examination or testing.
                      • Guarantee a cure or determine prescription medicine, dose, or duration from an image score.
                      • Guarantee a pet is healthy when no obvious skin concern is detected.
                      • Reliably predict a recovery date or treat checklist completion as recovery.
                      !

                      Important veterinary safety notice

                      DermaPaw AI provides image-based skin screening and educational care information for dogs and cats. Screening results are not a confirmed veterinary diagnosis. Similar skin symptoms can have different causes and may require professional examination or testing. Consult a licensed veterinarian for diagnosis and treatment. Do not start, stop, or change medication based solely on an AI result.
